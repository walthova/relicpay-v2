# Relic Pay — Marketing & Go-To-Market Plan

*Last updated: 2026-07-03. Owner: Walter. Executed daily by the RelicPay swarm (see SWARM.md).*

## Positioning

**"The only BNPL where your payment plan makes you money."**

Every other BNPL extracts from the buyer (late fees, credit damage, merchant fees passed through). Relic Pay inverts it: your locked installments earn yield while you pay. That single sentence is the hook in every pitch, post, and demo. Never lead with "decentralized" or "on Solana" — lead with the money the buyer earns.

Audience layers:
1. **Crypto-native buyers** (own USDC/SOL, hate selling) — earliest adopters, reachable on X/Farcaster
2. **Underrepresented communities locked out of credit** — the mission market; BNPL without credit checks
3. **Independent merchants** — get paid in full, no 6% BNPL fee; IBG Collection is merchant #1

## Phase 1 — Proof (weeks 1–4)

- Devnet demo live at relicpay.app with the v2 program: a stranger can complete a full BNPL flow with test USDC in under 3 minutes
- **IBG × Relic Pay pilot**: one real IBG Collection drop purchasable via Relic Pay checkout — this is the flagship story (cross-leverage: IBG gets a tech story, Relic Pay gets a real merchant)
- 90-second demo video (3Shapes Media production) walking the 5-step flow
- One-pager PDF for investors/partners, updated from v1 pitch deck

## Phase 2 — Presence (weeks 2–8, overlaps)

- X account: 3 posts/week — build-in-public threads (test counts, security fixes, Kamino progress), yield-math explainers, BNPL-industry critique
- Farcaster: same content, crypto-native framing
- Writing pipeline: 1 long-form piece / 2 weeks ("Why BNPL should pay you," "How on-chain escrow kills late fees") → Mirror + X threads
- Ship the browser extension to Chrome Web Store as a visible artifact

## Phase 3 — Ecosystem capital (weeks 4–12)

- **Solana hackathons**: submit to the next Colosseum round — yield-bearing BNPL is a strong consumer-track entry
- **Grants**: Superteam Earn / Solana Foundation grant applications (the repo, tests, and whitepaper are the application substance)
- Kamino ecosystem announcement once v2.1 CPI integration lands — their channels amplify integrations
- 3–5 LA independent brands as merchant pilots after IBG (streetwear/art adjacency, IBG's network)
