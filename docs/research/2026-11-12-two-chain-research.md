## Why Morpho, not Aave v3, on Base

1. **One interface, any curator.** Morpho vaults are ERC-4626 natively, so the escrow integration is the standard `deposit` / `redeem` pair. The same adapter works for any Morpho USDC vault and for any other ERC-4626 vault. Aave v3 would need a Pool-specific adapter (`supply` / `withdraw` plus aToken rebasing math).
2. **Depth on Base.** Public reporting describes Morpho as the largest USDC lending venue on Base, and Coinbase's onchain loan products are built on it. I did not re-check TVL figures today, so confirm with DefiLlama before mainnet. Depth matters for the exit-liquidity risk the escrow carries.
3. **Testnet is a wash.** Neither venue offers Circle USDC on Base Sepolia (Aave's test market uses its own token), so testnet uses a mock ERC-4626 vault either way. Tests cover harvest, loss and vault-change paths.
4. **Risk to flag.** Morpho vault risk depends on the curator, and an ERC-4626 vault can impose withdrawal limits. Before mainnet, pick one curated vault, record it in governance, and test `maxWithdraw` under stress (open question Q4).
