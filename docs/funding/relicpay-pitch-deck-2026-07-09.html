Problem

Traditional BNPL turns checkout timing into consumer debt.

BuyersPay late fees, lose credit flexibility, or sell crypto at bad timing.
MerchantsDepend on legacy BNPL rails and fees to reach installment buyers.
Crypto holdersHave assets, but no checkout-native way to use them without selling.
Solution

Relic Pay turns locked installments into productive escrow.

  • Buyer chooses an IBG item.
  • USDC locks in a Solana PDA escrow.
  • Installments settle by program rules.
  • Yield is simulated today, Kamino next.
Why Solana

Checkout breaks if settlement is slow or expensive.

Low-cost updatesInstallments, escrow state, and events need frequent transactions.
Composable yieldUSDC escrow can route into Kamino Lend through CPI.
Wallet-native UXNo bank account, credit bureau, or card network gate.
