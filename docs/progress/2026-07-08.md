**Key finding (resolves the 07-07 blocker):** yesterday's failure was an
*optional* account with no `seeds` constraint — Anchor's TS client only
auto-resolves accounts whose PDA is fully derivable from the IDL (constant
seeds or seeds referencing already-known accounts/args). `ProtocolConfig`'s
seed is the bare constant `["protocol_config"]`, so adding it as a
**required** `UncheckedAccount` with a `seeds` constraint works: the client
auto-derives and injects it even though the frozen `characterization.ts`
never lists it in `.accounts()`. Confirmed empirically — frozen suite still
passes untouched.
